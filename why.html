Why choose cheer?

Endless reasons...

Someone might choose to do cheer for a lot of great reasons—it's more than just chants and pom-poms. It’s a sport that builds confidence, strength, and community, while also being fun and exciting.

First, cheerleading is a great way to stay physically active. It includes a mix of dance, gymnastics, and strength training, so it helps improve flexibility, coordination, and endurance. If someone likes to move, perform, and stay fit, cheer can be a perfect fit.

Second, it teaches important life skills. Being on a cheer team builds teamwork, discipline, and leadership. Cheerleaders have to trust and rely on each other—especially in stunts—so it creates strong bonds and a sense of responsibility. Plus, performing in front of crowds boosts confidence and helps overcome stage fright.

Third, cheer is just a lot of fun. Whether you're performing at games, competing at nationals, or practicing with your squad, there's always energy and excitement. Many cheerleaders say their teammates become like family, and the memories they make together last a lifetime.
