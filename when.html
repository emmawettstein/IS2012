When is a good time to join?

Anytime!

Anytime is a good time to join cheer because it's one of those activities that welcomes all skill levels, encourages growth, and offers something valuable year-round—whether it’s building new friendships, learning cool skills, or just having fun.

One reason anytime is the right time to join is that cheer teams often have different roles and levels, so you don’t have to be an expert to start. Whether you're flexible, strong, love dancing, or just have great energy, there’s a place for you. Many people start with no experience and grow into amazing cheerleaders through practice and support.

Also, cheer runs year-round, with sideline seasons during football or basketball, and competitive cheer going full force at different times depending on the team. That means you don’t have to wait for a “perfect moment” to jump in—you can start whenever you’re ready and still learn, participate, and improve.

Plus, joining cheer is about more than the sport—it’s about becoming part of a positive, uplifting team environment. If you're ever looking to try something new, build confidence, or challenge yourself in a fun way, that’s the perfect time to join.

So whether it's the start of the school year, mid-season, or you're just feeling ready for something fresh—cheer is always ready for you.
