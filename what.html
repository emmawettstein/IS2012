What is cheer?

Everything you need to know about cheer...

Cheer as a sport—often called cheerleading—is a physically demanding activity that combines dance, gymnastics, stunts, jumps, and chants to support athletic teams, entertain crowds, and compete at various levels. Originally, cheerleading began as a way to lead crowds in cheering for sports teams, especially in schools and colleges. Over time, it evolved into a highly competitive sport of its own, with teams performing complex, choreographed routines at local, national, and even international competitions.

  1. Sport cheer typically includes two types:
    • All-Star Cheer: This type of cheerleading is focused on competition and is not affiliated with a school or college. All-Star teams train year-round and compete in various competitions, including national championships.
    • School Cheer: This type of cheerleading is typically associated with high schools and colleges. School cheerleaders support their teams at games and events, often performing routines that include stunts, jumps, and cheers.

Cheerleading requires strength, flexibility, coordination, teamwork, and trust, especially in stunts where safety is critical. It’s a fast-paced, athletic, and team-driven sport that pushes participants to be both performers and athletes.
